Editorial standards

  • Sources. Every article is based on one public source — a social media post, a paper, or a news item — and links to it. Social posts are embedded where possible.
  • How articles are produced. Articles are produced with AI-assisted tools, working within editorial rules set by LungDaily: what to cover, how to attribute, what to leave out. Before publication, each article is checked against its source for unsupported facts, inaccurate quotes, overstated headlines and medical advice. Articles that fail the check are held back and not published.
  • Attribution. Opinions are attributed to the people who hold them. Quotes are verbatim.
  • No medical advice. Nothing on this site is medical advice.
  • Independence. Sponsored content, if any, is always labelled as sponsored.
  • Corrections and removal. We correct errors quickly and remove articles on request from the people featured. See Corrections & opt-out.

Send us your news

Societies, journals, research groups, patient organisations and companies working on lung health: tell us what is new, and we will consider it for a short, sourced article.

What we cover

  • Research — new studies, reviews and guidelines on COPD, asthma, lung cancer and screening, bronchiectasis, ILD, TB, rehabilitation, air pollution, tobacco and vaping
  • Approvals & trials — regulatory decisions, trial readouts, new devices and medicines
  • Policy & air — national and global policy, clean air and tobacco control
  • People — appointments, elections, awards and honours in the respiratory field
  • Events — congresses, campaigns, calls for grants and awareness days

How to send it

Email {{ site.contact_email }} with:

  1. a link to your press release, announcement or paper (a public web page is best);
  2. two or three lines on why it matters;
  3. the embargo date and time, if there is one;
  4. a contact for questions.

On X you can also tag @{{ site.x_handle }}.

What happens next

  • If it fits, we publish a short article that links to your original and quotes it word for word.
  • We never publish embargoed material before the embargo lifts.
  • Not every item can be covered; we choose by relevance to the lung health community.
  • Spotted an error after publication? See corrections & opt-out.

Articles are produced with AI-assisted tools under our editorial standards and checked against the source before publication.
